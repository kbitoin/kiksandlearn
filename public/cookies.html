Cookie Policy

Effective: 1 October 2026

Essential storage

Hatchable uses session cookies for account security and sign-in. Browser storage remembers your light or dark theme, advertising preference and whether you declined a passkey offer. These preferences remain until you clear browser storage or change them.

Advertising and external media

The Google AdSense script contacts Google when a page loads. Advertising cookies and identifiers may support ad delivery, fraud prevention, measurement and personalization. Our default advertising consent signal is denied. The site's preference control is not a Google-certified consent management platform. The publisher must configure Google's certified Privacy & messaging flow before serving ads where that is required.

YouTube videos load only after you choose Load YouTube video. We use the privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com embed, but loading and playing media can still involve third-party data processing.

Manage your choices

Use Cookie settings in the footer to change advertising preferences. You can also delete cookies and local storage in your browser. Essential cookies cannot be disabled while remaining signed in. Google ad settings are available at https://myadcenter.google.com/.
