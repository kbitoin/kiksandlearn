Privacy Policy

Effective: 1 October 2026

Kiks & Learn is operated by Kiks to provide free learning and progress tracking. This policy explains the information used by this platform.

Information we use

Hatchable manages account email addresses, session cookies, verification codes, passkeys and, when enabled, Google sign-in profile details. Kiks & Learn stores your account identifier, enrollments, lesson completion, best quiz scores, accumulated active study time, comments, likes and recorded shares. Optional profile fields are name, age band and country. We do not request exact location, a birth date or biometric data. Your device manages passkey biometrics.

Purposes and access

We use this information to operate accounts, save progress, show learning recommendations and preparation milestones, prevent abuse and understand learner engagement. The owner can see learner progress and voluntarily supplied demographic fields. Comments are public; do not post confidential information. We do not sell learner profile data.

Service providers and advertising

Hatchable hosts the app and its database and delivers login emails. Google AdSense code is installed. Google and advertising partners may use cookies or identifiers to serve and measure ads, including personalization where valid consent is obtained. Google Fonts receives network requests for typefaces. Loading an embedded YouTube video contacts YouTube. External learning sites apply their own policies. Providers may process information outside your country.

Your choices and requests

You may leave demographic fields blank and change advertising preferences through Cookie settings in the footer. To request access, correction, export or deletion, use the privacy request form on this page while signed in. We retain learning records while your account is active unless deletion is requested; provider security logs follow provider retention rules. Essential service records may be retained where necessary for security or legal obligations.

Advertising controls

Manage Google ad personalization at https://myadcenter.google.com/ and review Google's partner-site practices at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Some third-party vendors may also use cookies. Browser settings can remove cookies, but removing essential cookies signs you out.

Children and updates

This platform is intended for adult learners and career switchers. If a child has supplied personal information without appropriate consent, contact us through the request form so we can address it. We update this policy when practices change and publish the effective date here.
